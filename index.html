How I approach a task

Careful. Consistent. Repeatable.

STEP 01

Read the brief

Understand task rules, labels, edge cases and delivery requirements before starting.

STEP 02

Work precisely

Keep labels and edits consistent; avoid guessing when instructions are unclear.

STEP 03

Review the output

Check missed objects, boundaries, accidental edits and naming consistency.

STEP 04

Apply feedback

Correct issues quickly and use feedback to improve the next batch of work.
